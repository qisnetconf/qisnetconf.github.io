About QISNet

QISNet 2027 provides an interdisciplinary forum for researchers and practitioners exploring how quantum science and technology can transform future communication, networking, distributed computing, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.

The workshop focuses on the convergence of quantum computing, quantum artificial intelligence, quantum communications, and quantum security with next-generation computer networks and distributed systems, and welcomes theoretical, experimental, and systems-oriented work, including hybrid quantum-classical approaches.

Overview

Scope of the workshop and the topics we invite contributions on.

Quantum computing and quantum machine learning may enable new approaches to network optimization, resource allocation, traffic analysis, distributed intelligence, and complex decision-making. Quantum communication technologies — including quantum key distribution, entanglement distribution, quantum repeaters, and emerging quantum-network architectures — introduce fundamentally new models for secure and distributed communication. At the same time, advances in quantum computing motivate a transition toward post-quantum cryptography, crypto-agility, and quantum-resilient network infrastructures.

The workshop places a strong emphasis on communications and networking, including architectures, protocols, algorithms, security mechanisms, intelligent resource-management methods, experimental systems, testbeds, and applications that connect quantum technologies with computer communications and networks.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Quantum Computing and Quantum AI for Networks
    • Quantum computing for communication and network optimization
    • Quantum algorithms for routing, scheduling, resource allocation, and network design
    • Quantum machine learning and quantum AI for networks
    • Hybrid quantum-classical AI for networked and distributed systems
    • Quantum-enhanced network traffic analysis, prediction, and anomaly detection
    • Quantum-enabled optimization for edge, cloud, and distributed computing
  • Quantum Communication Networks and the Quantum Internet
    • Quantum communication networks and architectures
    • Quantum internet architectures, protocols, and services
    • Quantum key distribution networks and secure key management
    • Entanglement generation, distribution, routing, and resource management
    • Quantum repeaters, quantum memories, and network control
    • Quantum network simulation, modeling, emulation, and testbeds
    • Classical-quantum network integration and control planes
    • Quantum networking for data centers, edge systems, IoT, and cyber-physical systems
  • Quantum-Safe Security and Post-Quantum Cryptography
    • Post-quantum cryptography for communication and network systems
    • Crypto-agility and migration to quantum-safe network infrastructures
    • Quantum-resilient authentication, identity, and trust management
    • Secure protocol design for quantum and post-quantum networks
    • Quantum threat modeling, risk assessment, and network security
    • AI-assisted security for quantum and post-quantum systems
  • Quantum Sensing, Distributed Intelligence, and Systems
    • Quantum sensing and networked quantum sensors
    • Quantum-enhanced distributed computing and federated or collaborative intelligence
    • Resource management and performance evaluation in quantum-enabled networks
    • Reliability, scalability, interoperability, and standardization of quantum networks
    • Experimental platforms, prototypes, applications, and use cases for quantum-enabled networks

Submission Guidelines

  • Length & Format: Papers are limited to 6 pages in the standard IEEE camera-ready format (double-column, 10-pt font).
  • Content: Submissions should include an abstract, keywords, and the e-mail address of the corresponding author.
  • Originality: Manuscripts should report original, unpublished research. Final requirements will follow the ICCCN 2027 workshop submission, formatting, registration, and presentation policies.
  • Review: All submitted papers will be peer reviewed by the Technical Program Committee.
  • Author Commitment: Submission of a paper is regarded as an undertaking that, should the paper be accepted, at least one author will register for and attend the workshop to present the work.
  • Publication: Accepted and presented workshop papers are expected to be included in the ICCCN conference proceedings and submitted for publication in IEEE Xplore, subject to the policies and technical co-sponsorship arrangements announced by ICCCN 2027.

Template: IEEE Conference Template (US letter, double-column). The submission link and detailed ICCCN 2027 submission instructions will be posted when available.
